Why the length matters more than the words

Placeholder copy that is always the same length hides every layout problem you are trying to find.

Buttons

Two words minimum, always

Design with the longest label you expect, not the shortest. “Save” fits everywhere; “Save and continue” is the string that breaks the row.

Cards

Titles wrap differently

A 40-character title wraps to two lines on mobile and one on desktop. Test both, or your grid will jump when real content arrives.

Translation

Plan for +30%

German and Finnish strings routinely run 30% longer than English; CJK runs shorter but needs different line-height. A layout that only fits one language is not localised, it is translated.

Honest limits

  • Line estimates are approximateThe tool reports a 60-character line width as a rough guide. Real line breaks depend on font, size, tracking and container width.
  • English onlyAll output is Latin filler. For RTL, CJK or accented-language testing, use a real string set from that language.
  • Not a copywriting shortcutShipping lorem ipsum to production is a content failure, not a design one. Replace every block before launch.

Questions this page answers

Straight answers, with the limits stated. Every claim here is checkable against the sources linked above.

What are the words actually from?

The standard passage is a scrambled excerpt of Cicero’s De finibus bonorum et malorum, in use as typesetting filler since the 1500s. It is public domain. This generator reassembles common lorem words rather than quoting a fixed paragraph.

Can I get exactly 120 characters for a meta description?

Use word mode and regenerate until the count lands near your target, or paste the output into an editor and trim. The character count updates live under the box.

Should I design with lorem ipsum?

For layout exploration, yes. For anything a stakeholder will approve or a user will test, use realistic copy — placeholder text makes bad hierarchy look acceptable.

Does the generator send anything to a server?

No. The word list is embedded in the page and generation runs in your browser.
